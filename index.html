Google Calendar Sync Guide

Keep your reminders consistent across all devices (iOS, Android, Mac, Windows) by connecting your scheduled items to Google Calendar.

Option 1: Export All Tasks to .iCal (.ics) File

Download an ics calendar file containing all current schedule items and import it directly into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.

Option 2: Individual Quick Add Links

Each task card on your dashboard has an instant "Add to Google Calendar" button () that creates an event link with automatic popup reminders on your smartphone or desktop.

How to import .ics to Google Calendar:

  1. Download the .ics file above.
  2. Open Google Calendar on your web browser.
  3. Click the Gear icon () in the top right → Settings.
  4. In the left sidebar, click Import & export.
  5. Upload your .ics file and select the destination calendar.
